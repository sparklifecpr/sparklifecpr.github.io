Policies and important information

Review these terms before registering. Finalize all policy language before launch.

Cancellation and rescheduling

Sample policy: Registrations may be transferred to another available class when notice is provided at least 48 hours before the scheduled start time. Cancellations within 48 hours and no-shows may be nonrefundable. SparkLife may reschedule a class due to instructor illness, low enrollment, severe weather, or unsafe conditions. In that event, registered students may transfer or request a refund.

Late arrival

Students arriving after required instruction has begun may need to reschedule to ensure course requirements can be completed.

Privacy

SparkLife collects registration and contact information for scheduling, payment coordination, customer service, attendance, and training-related communication. Card information is processed by the payment provider and is not stored on this website.

Accessibility and accommodations

Students should contact SparkLife before class to discuss reasonable training accommodations. Some courses include physical skills practice that may be required for completion.

Disclaimer

Course availability, credential eligibility, and completion requirements depend on the selected course and applicable provider standards. Website content does not replace professional medical advice or emergency services. In an emergency, call 911.

Legal review recommended: These are starter policies, not legal advice. Adapt them to the business, credentialing organization, venue, insurance requirements, and Virginia law before publication.
