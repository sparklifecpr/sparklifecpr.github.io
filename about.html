SparkLifeCPR mission

Help more people feel ready to respond.

Emergencies are stressful, but preparation can make the next step clearer. SparkLifeCPR helps learners practice the actions they may need to take while emergency services are on the way.

Classes are designed to be organized, approachable, and relevant to everyday life, healthcare, caregiving, education, and the workplace.
